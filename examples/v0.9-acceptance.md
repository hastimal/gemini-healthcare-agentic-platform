## Evidence boundaries

NPPES may support identity, NPI, taxonomy, and reported location. It is not proof of “best” status, board certification, active/current licensure, good standing, clinical quality, or requested service availability. PubMed supplies general scientific context, not provider-specific claims. Public HAPI FHIR data validates interoperability paths, not provider quality.

Gemma/Ollama was materially slower in observed local acceptance runs; those durations are diagnostics only, not benchmarks or performance guarantees.

`CARE_PROGRAM_DISCOVERY` and `CLINICAL_TRIALS` are not claimed as implemented retrieval paths in v0.9. Multi-source “best doctor” ranking is future work.
